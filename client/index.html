How to play
  1. Throw — Rock, Paper, Scissors. 5 seconds. Draws replay.
  2. Slap — the throw winner must SLAP, the loser must DODGE. 3 seconds. Nobody tells you which one you are.
  3. Slap lands before the dodge → dodger loses a heart. Dodge first → nobody loses one.
  4. Wrong move or frozen stiff → you lose a heart. Three hearts each. Last hand standing wins.
